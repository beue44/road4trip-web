Privacy policy

Road4Trip

The short version: Road4Trip collects nothing and sends nothing.

What data is collected

None. Road4Trip does not collect, store or transmit any personal data to the developer or to third parties.

There is no user account, no registration, no analytics tools, no advertising and no trackers.

The app does use iCloud – but your own. Everything Road4Trip stores sits in your own Apple Account, not on a server run by the developer. The developer has no access to it and does not learn that this data exists.

Your routes and trips

The routes, stops, dates, notes and ratings you enter are stored on your iPhone.

If you are signed in to iCloud, Road4Trip additionally syncs them between your own devices – iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch. Apple’s privacy terms for iCloud apply.

Photos are not transferred by default. Whether they are included is up to you, under Settings → Sync.

Without signing in to iCloud everything stays on the device, and the app keeps working in full.

When you share a trip

If you share a trip via “Plan with others”, Road4Trip stores a copy of that one trip in your iCloud and grants access to the people you invite. Name, notes, date and the stops are transferred – no photos, no history and no other routes.

Those invited can suggest stops and vote. These contributions are visible to everyone involved in the trip.

Sharing only happens when you trigger it, and can be revoked at any time – through the same sheet you used to invite. The developer of Road4Trip is not part of this share and cannot see it.

Backup in iCloud Drive

If you turn backups on, Road4Trip stores a copy of your routes in your personal iCloud Drive – in the “Road4Trip” folder. A switch decides whether photos are included.

This data stays in your own Apple Account. It does not go to the developer of Road4Trip and not to third parties; Apple’s privacy terms for iCloud apply.

You can delete stored backups yourself at any time in the Files app.

Calendar

If you add a trip as an event, Road4Trip asks for permission to write events – not to read them. So the app does not see your calendar.

Location

Road4Trip can use your location to show you on the map. The location is processed on the device only, never stored and never transmitted.

You can withdraw the permission in the iPhone settings at any time – the app remains usable.

Place search and route calculation

When you search for a place or a route is calculated, these requests go through Apple’s maps service. Apple’s privacy terms apply.

The provider of Road4Trip learns nothing about them.

Elevation gain (swisstopo)

When Road4Trip calculates the ascent and descent of a route, the app sends the coordinates of the calculated route to the map service of the Federal Office of Topography (swisstopo). Only elevation values come back.

Only coordinates are transmitted – no name, no identifier, nothing about you or your device. This happens only for trips within Switzerland and only for hiking, walking and cycling.

The privacy policy of swisstopo applies.

Navigation in Apple Maps and Google Maps

When you start a route, Road4Trip hands the coordinates of your stops to the maps app you chose. From that moment on, its privacy terms apply.

This handover only happens when you trigger it yourself.

Your rights

Since the developer collects no personal data, there are no records held by them about which information could be given or which could be deleted.

Control over your content lies entirely with you: on your device, and in your Apple Account. What is stored there can be viewed and deleted at any time in the iPhone settings under “Apple Account → iCloud”.

Changes

Should future versions gain further features that let data leave the device, this statement will be adjusted beforehand and pointed out in the app.
